    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in order. Story first — the chapter and mission trophies come free with it — then side strands, activities, collectibles, and finally the long grinds and the 100% completion gold.

      Built from the official descriptions: chapter and mission trophies (Chapters 1–4, the finale, Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory), the 8 stranger strands, the counted activities (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone races, 20 races, 18 meditation spots) and the counted collectibles (3 card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 8 garages).

      Missable trophies — read this first

      Good news: no trophy on the official list is permanently missable in a single playthrough — story missions and side strands can be finished whenever they appear, and the counted grinds are cumulative. The 9 trophies below are the ones that need a specific setup in a single attempt, so they are the only ones you can fail and have to retry. Do them carefully, in the order shown.
